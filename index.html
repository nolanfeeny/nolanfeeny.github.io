About

I study risk of systems people depend on: supply chains, communication networks, other infrastructure, and how to make them more resilient.

I’m a Ph.D. student in Industrial & Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan, where I also earned my B.S.E. and M.S.E. My work combines risk analysis, simulation, game theory, machine learning, and optimization.

I’m on the 2026–27 academic job market and expect to complete my Ph.D. in 2027.
